### H2: Frequently Asked Questions

**How far in advance should I book event pod rentals?**
Book 4–6 weeks before your event for standard configurations. For 10+ pods or peak season events (CES, SEMA, Dreamforce), book 8–12 weeks ahead to guarantee availability.

**Do event pods require special venue infrastructure?**
No. ZenSpace pods require only a standard power outlet (110V) and a flat floor surface. We coordinate with venue operations teams on delivery logistics.

**Can event pods be branded with our company logo?**
Yes. ZenSpace offers custom branding on windows, walls, doors, table wraps, and exterior digital screens. Branding packages start at $200 per graphic panel.

**Are ZenSpace pods ADA-compliant?**
Yes. The Element ADA pod offers wider doorways, accessible interior layout, and all standard smart features. All ZenSpace pods are UL-certified for electrical safety.

**Do you offer multi-event contracts?**
Yes. Event organizers and venues running multiple events per year can negotiate annual contracts with preferred pricing and priority scheduling.
