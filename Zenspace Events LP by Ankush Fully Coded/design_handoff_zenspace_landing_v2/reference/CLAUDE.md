## 6. Service Model — Full-Service Process

ZenSpace handles every step. The client approves decisions and provides power.

### Before the Event

**Step 1 — Event Alignment & Onboarding**  
A dedicated ZenSpace specialist guides the client through a dynamic Onboarding Form (adapts to each event's specific needs). Captures pod count, schedule, venue floor plan, branding requirements. The form is "the single source of truth for all teams involved." Returning clients use pre-saved preferences for faster setup.

**Step 2 — Design & Booking Setup Approval**  
- *Pod Branding:* Design team reviews assets for print readiness, creates visualizations, gets client approval before production
- *Booking Page:* Event-specific branded booking page configured (logos, colors, imagery, time slots — free or paid). Branded QR code ready before event day. Both require client approval before launch.

**Step 3 — Logistics & On-Site Installation**  
ZenSpace coordinates truck delivery to venue. ZenSpace crew handles unwrapping, installation, connections, testing, and finalization. Show management provides: electrical power (5 Amp per pod) and assists with unloading via forklift or pallet jack. ZenSpace does not require additional venue coordination beyond this.

### During & After the Event

**Step 4 — Live Support + Real-Time Dashboard**  
ZenSpace provides live operational support throughout. Remote content updates can be pushed to pods instantly. Dashboard (if booking software enabled) shows: occupancy monitoring, open-door alerts, loitering detection, item notifications. Zero extra staff required from the client.

**Step 5 — Removal + Full Utilization Report**  
ZenSpace handles complete pod dismantling and removal post-event. Client receives a detailed Booking Utilization Report: total bookings, utilization rates, and full lead generation data (names, companies, emails, phone numbers).

## 8. Voice & Messaging Guidelines

### Tone: Calm. Precise. Empowering.

ZenSpace doesn't shout. The brand speaks with the quiet confidence of someone who knows exactly what they're doing. Every word is intentional — no filler, no hype.

- **Clear over clever** — explain complex technology in ways that feel effortless
- **Confident, not arrogant** — show what's possible, don't compete loudly
- **Warm but professional** — a partner, not a vendor; human, not corporate
- **Forward-looking** — talk about what could be, not just what is

**Sound like:** A trusted expert who opens doors you didn't know were there.  
**Never sound like:** A salesperson, a tech manual, or a company trying too hard to impress.

### Approved Brand Language

| Use this | Not this |
|---|---|
| "Smart" / "tech-enabled" | "IoT-enabled" |
| "Pod" / "SmartPod" / "meeting pod" | "booth" |
| "Soundproof" / "acoustic privacy" | "quiet" (too weak) |
| "UL-certified" | Just "certified" |
| "Refined by experience" | Generic "industry leader" |
| "Full-service" | "turnkey" (fine but less ZenSpace) |
| "5 Amp outlet" | "power connection" (too vague) |
| "Private meeting space" | "conference room" (wrong context) |

### Key Phrases to Use
- "On-Demand Workspace. Anytime. Anyplace."
- "Refined by experience"
- "We handle everything. You provide power."
- "Only requirement: a 5 Amp outlet per pod"
- "Zero burden on your team"
- "Turn pods into [revenue assets / brand touchpoints / high-impact destinations]"
- "Real conversations, not just brochures"
- "Bring calm to your event"
- "Offer the pod. Win the spec." (GSC / exhibit design context)
- "Give your attendees somewhere to actually think." (corporate context)

### Things to Avoid
- "IoT-enabled" — not brand voice; use "smart" or "tech-enabled"
- Calling pods "booths" — they are pods, SmartPods, or private meeting spaces
- "State-of-the-art" — generic filler
- Overpromising on customization without noting the branding approval step
- Removing the "5 Amp" detail — it is a core proof of simplicity
- Vague claims without numbers ("significantly more leads" → "2.4× more leads")
