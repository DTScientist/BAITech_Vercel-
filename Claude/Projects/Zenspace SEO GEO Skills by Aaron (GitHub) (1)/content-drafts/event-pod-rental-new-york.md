Each pod displays a QR code on its exterior digital signage. Attendees scan the code and self-book a time slot — no app download required. Booking confirmation is delivered instantly. Organizers and exhibitors can view all bookings in real time via the ZenSpace analytics dashboard.

**Can event pods be branded with our company logo?**

Yes. ZenSpace offers the Element ADA Pod, which meets ADAAG (Americans with Disabilities Act Accessibility Guidelines) standards. It features wider doorways and a 4-person capacity. For public-facing events in New York, ADA-accessible pod options are available and recommended as part of multi-pod deployments. The Element ADA Pod is UL-certified.

**What is the sound attenuation level of ZenSpace pods?**
ZenSpace SmartPods provide -29dB sound attenuation. At the Javits Center and similar large-format venues, this means conversations inside a pod are not audible to attendees standing on the adjacent trade show floor — providing genuine privacy for sales meetings, demos, and confidential discussions.

**What happens if I need to move a pod during the event?**
On-site pod relocation is available at $500 per pod, one-time fee. ZenSpace handles all physical relocation. If you anticipate needing to adjust pod positions, flag this during the booking process so it can be built into your floor logistics plan.
